How We Work

  1. Free site visit: We inspect the floor, check cracks, moisture and existing coatings, and note how the area is used: forklifts, trolleys, chemicals or foot traffic.
  2. System and quote: You get a written quote with the recommended system, thickness and the number of working days.
  3. Surface preparation: Grinding or shot blasting, crack and joint repair, so the coating bonds to sound concrete.
  4. Coating and marking: Primer, body coat and top coat, followed by walkway, bay and safety line marking where needed.
  5. Handover: The floor is cured before traffic, and you get cleaning and maintenance guidance.
