What Affects Epoxy Flooring Cost?

  • Total floor area to be coated

  • Condition of the existing surface (repairs, leveling, crack filling)

  • Number of coating layers and thickness required

  • Type of system — standard, anti-slip, ESD or chemical-resistant

  • Site location and accessibility for material & equipment

Frequently Asked Questions — Epoxy Flooring Cost

What is the cost of epoxy flooring per square foot in India?

Basic epoxy flooring typically costs ₹35-₹70 per sq ft, self-leveling epoxy ₹80-₹150 per sq ft, and specialised systems like anti-slip or ESD flooring ₹150-₹250+ per sq ft, depending on surface condition, coating thickness and location.

Does epoxy flooring price vary by city in Maharashtra?

Prices can vary slightly by city due to transport and labour costs, but CoatCraft Solutions offers consistent, transparent pricing across Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, Nashik, Aurangabad, Kolhapur, Solapur and Thane. Use our free calculator or call 7745889111 for a location-specific quote.

What factors affect epoxy flooring cost?

Key factors include floor area, condition of the existing surface (repairs/leveling needed), coating thickness, type of system (standard, anti-slip, ESD, chemical-resistant), and site accessibility.

Is anti-slip or ESD epoxy flooring more expensive than standard epoxy flooring?

Yes. Anti-slip and ESD (electrostatic dissipative) flooring use specialised additives and multi-layer application, so they typically cost more than standard epoxy flooring, usually in the ₹150-₹250+ per sq ft range.
